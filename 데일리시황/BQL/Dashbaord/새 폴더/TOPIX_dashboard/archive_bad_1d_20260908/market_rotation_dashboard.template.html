Data Coverage

The source currently covers 2026-09-02 through 2026-09-07. One-day changes use the previous weekday observation. Five-day changes use five prior weekday observations, falling back to the earliest available observation until five complete sessions exist. EPS changes rebuild constituent earnings as EPS × implied shares, where implied shares equal market cap ÷ price, and compare the summed earnings amounts. Positive observations are used, constituent earnings changes are capped at ±100%, and group contributions use base-period earnings weights. P/E equals total market cap divided by total earnings; observations below 1x are excluded as data-quality outliers. Price attribution uses start-date market-cap weights and splits the EPS×P/E interaction equally so EPS and P/E contributions sum to price return. Operating-margin observations outside ±200% are excluded from weighted averages. Price returns exclude dividends.
