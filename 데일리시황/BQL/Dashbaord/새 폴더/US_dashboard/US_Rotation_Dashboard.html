Valuation vs Forward Fundamentals

Data Coverage

EPS changes rebuild constituent earnings as EPS × implied shares, where implied shares equal market cap ÷ price, and then compare the summed earnings amounts. Positive observations are used and stock-level earnings changes are capped at ±100% for 4W/13W comparisons. Group contributions use base-period earnings weights. In comparison charts, 13W EPS change is linearly converted to a 4W basis (13W × 4/13) and compared with actual 4W change. Price & Attribution II uses the latest date on which a material share of constituent prices actually changed; 1D and 5D EPS changes are aligned to the same active-price sessions. FY1/FY2 lines show aggregate forward-earnings estimate changes since 2026-01-01. P/E equals total market cap divided by total earnings; observations below 1x are excluded as data-quality outliers. Price attribution uses start-date market-cap weights; P/E contribution is the residual multiple change implied by price return and earnings change, with the EPS×P/E interaction split equally so the two contributions sum to price return. Operating-margin observations outside ±200% are excluded from weighted averages. Price returns and the S&P 500 heatmap exclude dividends. Valuation history is limited to the dates available in the raw file; the selected window determines the mean and standard deviation in Valuation I.
